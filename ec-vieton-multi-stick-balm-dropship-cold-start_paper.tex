\begin{abstract}
Background: The introduction of premium K-beauty formulations into tropical markets requires rigorous assessment of both product stability and logistical viability. This study evaluates the INCELLDERM Vieton Multi Stick Balm in Ecuador, focusing on its performance under high-humidity coastal conditions and the adoption barriers within a 'Zero-Inventory Drop-Shipping' business model for independent planners. Methods: A stratified survey of 200 participants (N=200) across Guayaquil, Quito, and Cuenca was conducted using Likert scales, NPS metrics, and multi-select behavioral analysis. Quantitative Results: The composite mean score was 3.65 with a Top-2-Box satisfaction rate of 64.2\%. While absorption speed (mean 3.93, T2B 89.5\%) and ingredient efficacy perception (Niacinamide/Camellia combination, mean 3.95, T2B 88.0\%) were highly rated, texture stability in high humidity showed moderate confidence (mean 3.56, T2B 56.0\%). Crucially, the Net Promoter Score (NPS) for the drop-shipping program was -40.0, driven by significant detractors (42.5\%) citing quality control uncertainty (88.0\% selection rate). Strategic Implications: Independent planners prioritize 'Clean INCI Transparency' (91.5\%) and 'Patent-Backed Hydration' (90.0\%) as key marketing angles. However, cold-start success is hindered by consumer trust deficits and logistics complexity. Recommendations include implementing strict QC protocols and leveraging digital assets to mitigate perceived risks in the absence of physical samples.

\vspace{0.4em}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} RIMAN Skincare, INCELLDERM, Vieton Multi Stick Balm, Ecuador Market Entry, Drop-Shipping Logistics, Microclimate Adaptation, Korean Dermatology
\end{abstract}

\section{1. Formulation Architecture \& Study Methodology}
The INCELLDERM Vieton Multi Stick Balm leverages proprietary technologies including Giant BYoungPool, Jeju Lava Energy Water, and an 8-layer Hyaluronic Acid complex. To assess its viability in the Ecuadorian market, particularly for independent beauty planners operating under a no-auto-ship, zero-inventory model, this study employed a mixed-methods approach. The sample consisted of 200 respondents stratified by occupation (B2B vs. C-End), income tier, and geographic microclimate (Guayaquil’s tropical savanna at 79\% RH, Quito’s equatorial highland at 68\% RH, and Cuenca’s Andean mild climate at 70\% RH). The methodology focused on quantifying product performance against local environmental stressors and evaluating the psychological and logistical barriers to adopting a drop-shipping supply chain.
\section{2. Quantitative Metrics \& Microclimate Adaptation}
Empirical data reveals a nuanced performance profile. The overall composite mean was 3.65, with a Top-2-Box (T2B) agreement of 64.2\%. In terms of sensory attributes, the balm excelled in absorption speed, achieving a mean of 3.93 and a T2B of 89.5\%, indicating that users find it suitable for quick application without greasy residue in tropical climates. The scent profile, derived from Rosa Damascena Flower Water, also received high marks (mean 3.83, T2B 81.0\%). However, concerns regarding physical stability emerged; only 56.0\% of respondents agreed that the stick texture remains stable under typical high-humidity conditions (mean 3.56). Furthermore, while the combination of Niacinamide and Camellia Japonica Seed Oil was perceived as effective for brightening and barrier repair (mean 3.95, T2B 88.0\%), confidence in the antioxidant capabilities of Jeju Lava Energy Water and Sea Buckthorn Water was lower (mean 3.29, T2B 38.0\%), suggesting a need for stronger educational messaging on these specific patented ingredients.
\section{3. Cross-Demographic, MBTI \& B2B/C-End Stratification}
Segmentation analysis highlights distinct behavioral patterns. B2B stakeholders, particularly aesthetic clinic owners, reported higher satisfaction (mean 3.80) compared to C-End consumers. Income tiers correlated positively with satisfaction, with Affluent/High Net Worth individuals scoring 3.81 versus Lower-Middle Income at 3.54. Psychographic profiling via MBTI revealed that pragmatic types (ESTJ, mean 3.75) and intuitive feelers (INFJ, mean 3.73) were more receptive to the product's value proposition than analytical skeptics (INTP, mean 3.58). Regarding the drop-shipping model, 88.0\% of respondents identified 'quality control uncertainty' as their primary concern. When asked about acceptable lead times in a cold-start scenario, 58.5\% accepted 7-14 days, while 28.0\% required under 7 days. Marketing preferences strongly favored 'Clean INCI Transparency' (91.5\%) and 'Patent-Backed Hydration' (90.0\%), whereas 'Tropical Climate Adaptation' was less critical (41.5\%). Barriers to adoption included 'Consumer Trust in New Brand' (100.0\% selection rate) and 'Complex Logistics Coordination' (97.0\%).
\section{4. Regulatory Compliance \& Regional Strategy}
Regulatory confidence remains a challenge, with only 37.0\% of respondents expressing certainty that the INCI list complies with all necessary cosmetic safety regulations for import and sale in Ecuador (mean 3.37). This aligns with the low NPS score of -40.0 for the drop-shipping program, where 42.5\% are detractors. To improve regional strategy, RIMAN must address the lack of physical samples (cited by 76.0\% as a barrier) and provide robust digital marketing assets (selected by 76.5\% as critical support). Flexible payment terms (86.0\%) and regular product updates (93.5\%) are also essential for sustaining sales without auto-ship commitments. Competitor benchmarking shows that planners primarily compare Vieton against 'K-Beauty Imported Sticks' (97.0\%), necessitating a clear differentiation strategy based on clinical efficacy and transparent sourcing rather than just price or novelty.

\section{Conclusion}
The INCELLDERM Vieton Multi Stick Balm demonstrates strong sensory appeal and efficacy perception in Ecuador, particularly regarding absorption and key active ingredients like Niacinamide. However, the zero-inventory drop-shipping model faces significant headwinds due to quality control anxieties and regulatory opacity, resulting in a negative NPS (-40.0). Success for independent planners hinges on mitigating these risks through enhanced transparency, faster logistics guarantees, and targeted education on patented hydration technologies. Strategic adjustments should focus on building consumer trust via digital assets and addressing the specific humidity-related stability concerns to unlock the full potential of this product in the Latin American K-beauty market.
